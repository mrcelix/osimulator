Pair a Bluetooth device

Connect headphones, a keyboard, a controller or any accessory.

Try it live in the simulator →

iOS

  1. Put the accessory in pairing mode
  2. Open Settings → Bluetooth
  3. Turn Bluetooth on
  4. Tap the device under Other Devices

Android

  1. Settings → Connected devices
  2. Tap Pair new device
  3. Select your accessory
  4. Confirm the pairing code

macOS

  1. System Settings → Bluetooth
  2. Turn Bluetooth on
  3. Wait for the device to appear
  4. Click Connect next to it

Windows

  1. Settings → Bluetooth & devices
  2. Click Add device → Bluetooth
  3. Choose your accessory from the list
  4. Follow the on‑screen prompts
